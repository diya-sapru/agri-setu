# 🌾 AgriSetu

### Sell Where. Sell When. Sell Better.

AgriSetu is an AI-powered agricultural market intelligence
and buyer-matching platform designed to help farmers and FPOs
make better selling decisions.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Farmers often lack access to:

- Current market prices
- Reliable buyers
- Buyer demand
- Quality requirements
- Transport and storage information
- Payment reliability

This can result in information asymmetry, weak bargaining power,
and lower price realization.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

AgriSetu combines:

- 📊 Market prices
- 📈 Price trends
- 🤝 Buyer demand
- 🌾 Crop quality
- 🚚 Logistics
- 🏬 Storage
- ⭐ Buyer reliability

to recommend:

> **WHERE + WHEN + TO WHOM a farmer should sell.**

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- 📊 Market Intelligence
- 🤖 Smart Sell Recommendation
- 🤝 Buyer Matching
- 🌾 FPO Lot Aggregation
- ⭐ Buyer Trust Score
- 💰 Digital Offers
- 🚚 Logistics Coordination
